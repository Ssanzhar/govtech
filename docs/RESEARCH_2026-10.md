## 1. Bottom line

1. **There is no public Russian or Kazakh scam-call transcript corpus anywhere.** Re-confirmed
   against 632 Kazakh HF datasets, Zenodo, Figshare, Kaggle, IEEE DataPort, Mendeley, LDC,
   GitHub and the KZ/RU portals. Stop looking for one. What exists instead: **real scam
   scripts in court verdicts** (Kazakh and Russian, `office.sud.kz`; Russian in bulk via
   `sudrf.ru`), **real English legit-call negatives at scale** (91k real BPO transcripts, 873
   role-played bank/telecom/delivery/government dialogues), and a 10k-call real US honeypot
   corpus announced but not yet downloadable.
2. **The data lever is ours to pull: a Claude-written, grid-controlled corpus built from a v2
   taxonomy.** Because `shift` is Claude-written (D35), it stops being the honest number the
   moment Claude writes training data. The honest number must move to new splits generated by
   *other* sources (§3.2). This is the one methodological trap of the whole plan.
3. **Keep `multilingual-e5-base`; do not fine-tune the encoder before the deadline.** Nothing
   ≤ 300 MB beats it on Kazakh (MTEB `kaz_Cyrl`); EmbeddingGemma is weak on Kazakh. Every
   paper found shows encoder fine-tuning is what collapses under generator shift (BERT
   precision 0.128 → 0.041 on a temporal split; KoBERT 98.5 → 56.3 % adversarial). The design
   the literature validates is **ours**: cue-span primitives + a logical layer (ACL 2026
   Findings: F1 0.718 vs 0.223 for fine-tuned BERT, vs 0.224 for attention-MIL). So: more cues,
   more tactics, a scheme layer, calibrated thresholds — not a new encoder.
4. **The cheapest model win is size, not accuracy:** ~190 M of e5-base's 278 M parameters are
   the 250k-token vocabulary. Trimming it to kk/ru/en (~80–100k pieces) gives the same encoder
   at **~150–165 MB int8** (estimate; a published kk-only trim of e5-small shrank it 71 %).
5. **The taxonomy has real gaps, and they are the low-arousal ones.** Parcel / clinic / utility /
   pension "codes" (the #1 and #2 pretexts in Kazakhstan's 2026 wave), loan inducement
   («зеркальный кредит», ~1,300 fraudulent loans since January), cash-courier hand-off,
   "erroneous transfer — return it", job-task advance fees, voice-cloned relatives, and 19
   observed impersonation roles that three `impersonation_*` ids cannot carry. These carry no
   fear/urgency lexicon — the likeliest cause of the cross-generator recall loss.
6. **STT, corrected 2026-10-08:** the STT agent reported that we ship the obsolete
   `vosk-model-small-kz-0.15`. **Wrong** — `config.py` and `site/core/qorgan-config.json` have
   used `vosk-model-small-kz-0.42` since commit `4868659` (July). No Kazakh model swap is available.
7. **Vosklet cannot run inside an Android WebView**, including eGov Mobile's: WebView has no
   cross-origin isolation by design, so WASM threads fail. Single-threaded sherpa-onnx WASM
   does run there; a 25 MB int8 Russian streaming model from the Vosk authors and T-Bank's
   `T-one` (8 kHz telephony, WER 7.9 on real Russian calls) are ready for it. A joint KK+RU
   model with one vocabulary (`nvidia/stt_kk_ru_fastconformer_hybrid_large`, CC-BY-4.0) is the
   right long-term answer to mid-sentence code-switching; the literature says a joint model
   beats per-utterance voting exactly there.
8. **Kazakh is offline on no native stack** (iOS 27, Android Live Caption/Transcribe: Russian
   yes, Kazakh no). Any native eGov integration still means bring-your-own model.
9. **"Integrate into eGov" has no SDK, no mini-app store and no developer programme.** Every
   third-party feature in eGov Mobile was built in by NIT at a state body's request. The real
   route: a state sponsor + a **пилотный приказ**, backend in Kazakhstan, ГТС/accredited-lab
   security tests (list price ≈ 2.7–3 M ₸, 2–4 months), Smart Bridge agreement (paid since
   March 2025), NIT builds the thin UI. 6–12 months after a sponsor signs. What the team can
   do alone now: **eGov QR signing for verified reports** (live Smart Bridge service) and a
   **structured hand-off** of consented reports to the channels the Nov-2025 rules created
   (operator must check a complained-about number within 2 working days).
10. **Tooling:** `claude -p` and in-session workflows run on the Max pool (the "separate Agent
    SDK credit" is paused; never use `--bare`). Dynamic Workflows are the right primitive for
    generation (resumable, 16 concurrent agents). Install list in §7.

### 2.2 Model
- **Encoder** (MTEB `kaz_Cyrl`, SIB200 classification / Belebele / NTREX): e5-base
  0.611 / 0.757 / 0.924; e5-large-instruct 0.824 / 0.895 / 0.990 but 562 MB; gte-multilingual-base
  340 MB int8 and below e5-base on NTREX; EmbeddingGemma-300m Tatoeba kaz-eng 0.27 (e5-base
  0.756); jina-v3 CC-BY-NC. **Keep e5-base.**
- **Vocabulary trimming** (Ushio et al. 2023; `lm-vocab-trimmer`): same weights for every kept
  piece, so parity holds except where SentencePiece re-segments a rare word — measurable with
  the golden fixtures. Needs re-capture of the browser gate. **→ ADR.**
- **Fine-tuning:** SetFit is the only variant worth trying (contrastive pairs, hours on MPS),
  with ≥ 8 of 12 layers frozen and gated on the new held-outs; it replaces the embedder, so
  every fixture and the browser gate must be re-captured. Post-deadline.
- **Architecture:** attention-MIL confirmed a no-go (matches D36). Worth one small experiment
  later: a tiny GRU over the 15 per-utterance tactic posteriors + cue flags (tactic
  *trajectory* is predictive — Vishing-Tactics-Bench, StreamFraudNet). Nearest-prototype over
  a scheme bank is a fine **scheme-label** layer but never the risk gate.
- **FPR-first calibration:** choose the 0.59 / 0.49 pair with Learn-then-Test on a
  **negatives-only calibration set from a third source**; ~45 clean negatives with 0 FPs certify
  FPR ≤ 5 % at 90 % confidence. The 66-call `shift` cannot certify tighter.

### 2.3 Taxonomy v2 (three layers)
- **Layer 1 — tactics (what the heads learn).** Keep urgency, fear_threat, secrecy, otp_request,
  credentials_request, safe_account, payment_redirect, remote_access (lexicon += screen-share /
  video-call), prize_lottery, investment_scam, mule_recruitment, verification_ploy. Split the
  three `impersonation_*` into six: `imp_bank`, `imp_law_enforcement`, `imp_gov_service`
  (eGov/ЦОН/КГД/pension/clinic), `imp_regulator` (Нацбанк/Антифрод-центр/АРРФР),
  `imp_telecom_delivery_utility`, `imp_personal` (relative/boss/friend). Add five:
  `loan_inducement`, `cash_courier_handoff`, `refund_request`, `task_advance_fee`,
  `callback_instruction`. Negative classes += `legit_delivery_code` (real couriers do ask for
  an app code), `legit_refund_request`, `legit_employer_request`, `legit_clinic_appointment`,
  `legit_telecom_offer`, `legit_utility_notice` — the FPR guardrails for the new positives.
- **Layer 2 — role slot.** Deterministic lexicon extraction of one of 19 observed roles,
  surfaced verbatim («Звонящий представился {role}» / «Қоңырау шалушы өзін {role} деп
  таныстырды»). No learning, no FPR exposure, cheap, visible.
- **Layer 3 — scheme (call-level, rule-derived).** ~20 scenarios (`parcel_code`,
  `egov_eds_renewal`, `telecom_contract_expiry`, `tax_kgd_business`, `benefits_recalc_and_clinic`,
  `two_stage_escalation`, `mirror_loan`, `regulator_impersonation`, `erroneous_transfer`,
  `relative_in_trouble_voiceclone`, `fake_boss`, `job_task_advance_fee`,
  `marketplace_seller_phish`, `cash_declaration_courier`, …), each a hand-written logical
  expression over tactics + role + stage (hook / escalation / extraction / hand-off, plus a
  multi-call flag). Advice keyed by scheme, not tactic. Adding a scheme = a rule + advice, no
  retrain, unless it needs a new tactic.
- **Rule that binds it all (CLAUDE.md):** every lexicon addition forces a retrain and must ship
  with training data → the generator grid in §3.1 is built *from* this taxonomy, and both land
  in **one ADR**.

## 3. The two data decisions that matter

### 3.1 Generating with Claude without collapsing the corpus
- **Grid, not prompts:** scheme (~20) × claimed role (19) × register (kk / ru / kk-ru switch /
  en) × victim persona (age, region, occupation) × outcome (complies / refuses / hangs up /
  calls back) × channel (cold call / call-back after SMS / messenger-then-call) × stage × an
  explicit length target. Attributed prompting matches class-conditional quality at 5 % of
  the queries and attribute diversity drives downstream accuracy (AttrPrompt); Cosmopedia's
  topic × audience × style grid + dedup is the template; TeleAntiFraud's three-source pipeline
  is the closest published blueprint.
- **Size:** 1,500–2,500 dialogues, not 20k — the gain curve flattens fast (ACL 2026 Fig. 3).
- **QC gates, all automated:** schema validation (existing `Dialogue`); e5-cosine near-dup
  removal within class (`semhash`, start where today's train has no pairs > 0.92 — calibrate);
  ROUGE-L < 0.7 novelty vs the pool (Self-Instruct); **lexicon-leak check** — ≥ 30 % of
  positives per scheme must contain **no** hard-signal cue, or the embedding head never has to
  learn; **generator-fingerprint probe** — an LR that tells Claude rows from Gemini rows on
  scrubbed text; if it beats chance widely the style leak is real and the held-out design in
  §3.2 is mandatory; scrub fixed point (D45); disjointness from every eval split (as
  `shift_set.py` already does).
- **Mechanics:** a saved Dynamic Workflow that `pipeline()`s over the grid manifest in chunks of
  20–30 dialogues; each agent gets the JSON schema, never the lexicon, writes
  `data/synthetic/gen/<run>/<chunk>.jsonl` and returns `{chunk, n, sha256}`; idempotent chunks
  (skip if hash exists); runs scheduled right after a usage-window reset.

### 3.2 Keeping an honest held-out after the generator swap (→ ADR)
1. Freeze today's `shift` as **`shift_v1`** in the ledger: a *validation* split from now on,
   never the headline number again.
2. **`shift_local`** — ~100 calls written by an independent model family run offline on the
   Mac (`issai/LLama-3.1-KazLLM-1.0-8B` or `issai/Qwen3.5-4B-Kazakh` via llama.cpp): different
   pretraining, native Kazakh, free. No sight of corpus, prompts or lexicons (same protocol as
   D35).
3. **`court_authored`** — 50–100 calls hand-written by the team from the reported speech in KZ
   and RU court verdicts (§2.1), each citing its case. Real scripts, human register.
4. **`real_asr`** — 50–100 public KZ/RU scam recordings (journalist/victim uploads) transcribed
   with **our own** Vosk pipeline, inspection-ledgered. The only split that also tests ASR
   noise honestly. Legal basis recorded per `DATA_INTAKE.md` §8; never redistributed.
5. **`shift_v2`** (optional) — a few hundred Gemini-written calls if the key has any quota;
   roles swapped, Gemini becomes a held-out generator.
6. Human-authored negatives stay `authored_heldout`; the LTT calibration set (§2.2) is drawn
   from 3–4, negatives only.
The honest headline becomes the worst of `shift_local` / `court_authored` / `real_asr`, with
intervals, as `shift` is today.
