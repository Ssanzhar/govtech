# Qorğan × eGov Mobile — what "integrate into eGov" actually requires

Research memo, 2026-10-07. Confidence marks: **[A]** primary legal text / official NIT-NBK-ministry source; **[B]** reputable KZ media or secondary analysis; **[C]** vendor blog, inference, or single unverified mention. "Not found" means I searched (RU/KK/EN) and found nothing public.

## (a) Bottom line

There is no public developer programme, SDK or mini-app store for eGov Mobile: every third-party thing inside the app today (QazaqGaz Aimaq meter readings, the Prosecutor's fraud-awareness tab, Nomad Guard, the MVD "Закон и порядок" section) was put there by NIT itself at the request of a state body [A/B]. Since the Digital HQ's October-2025 "superapp" decision, state bodies may ship mobile services only through eGov Mobile and Aitu [B], and since 5 Sept 2026 those two plus eGov Business and Enbek carry a formal "special status" [B]. So "integrate into eGov Mobile" means, concretely: find a state sponsor (MVD, General Prosecutor's Office, or MAIDD's cyber-security bloc), get a **приказ о запуске пилотного проекта**, host the backend in Kazakhstan, pass ГТС/accredited-lab ИБ testing (≈2–3 M ₸ list price, 2–4 months), connect through Smart Bridge/ВШЭП under a tripartite agreement with NIT (paid since March 2025), and let NIT build the thin UI. The two things Qorğan can do alone, now, are (i) verified-identity reporting via eGov QR signing (already used by Otbasy Bank and sud.kz) and (ii) a structured hand-off of consented reports into the channels the law created in Nov 2025 (operator must check a complained-about number within 2 working days; Antifraud Center is building a "potential victims" base). Transcript-level, on-device detection of social engineering in KK/RU is not duplicated by anything in the ecosystem; number reputation, transaction blocking and link/leak checks are. Platform reality is unchanged: no third-party app on Android or iOS can read live call audio, so speakerphone-in-foreground or post-call analysis is the only honest in-app shape.

## 2. Smart Bridge and ШЭП: registering a system and publishing/consuming an API

- **Scale (2026):** 11,377 organisations, 2,953 services (2,091 state, 862 private), 4,671 integrations [B] — https://kapital.kz/tehnology/145821/ .
- **Legal basis for a non-state system.** Law № 418-V (ex-"Об информатизации", now "О кибербезопасности" after the Digital Code; amended by Law 326-VIII, in force 25.08.2026) art. 38(2): a non-state system that forms state resources needs a **ТЗ agreed with the authorised body**, positive **cybersecurity tests**, integration **only via the external gateway (ВШЭП)** and compliance with the Unified Requirements; art. 49: tests by ГТС or **accredited laboratories** [A] — https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/Z1500000418 , https://www.zakon.kz/obshestvo/6531950- . Unified Requirements (ПП № 832) para 2: ИБ provisions bind owners of non-state IS integrated with state IS; para 49: class-1/2 systems in data centres on RK territory; logs kept ≥3 years [A] — https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/P1600000832 .
- **Integration Rules (Order № 123 of 29.03.2018, as amended):** operator (NIT) checks the application in 3 working days; test-environment access within 10; joint testing up to 3 months; signed testing act reviewed in 3; publication to production within 10; required: application, technical description (auth, SSL), XSD/XML samples, **ИБ test protocols**; 30-second synchronous timeout [A] — https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V1800016777 .
- **Operational reality (NIT's channel):** contracts signed electronically in the Smart Bridge cabinet since March 2025; **paid model for B2B/G2B from 25 March 2025 under Order № 104/НҚ (12.03.2025)**; mandatory certificate validation/mTLS in production from 27 Feb 2026; endpoint moved to shep.gov.kz; B2G auto-escalation if the service owner is silent for 2 business days; contacts sayat.tulessov@nitec.kz, bagdat.kanybek@nitec.kz [A] — https://t.me/s/sb_shep . A July-2026 MinAI order under the Digital Code made paid access the legal default and introduced 2-business-day auto-approval; tariffs to be set separately by NIT; the Financial Association protested [B] — https://exclusive.kz/minii-perevodit-dostup-k-egov-dlja-biznesa-na-platnuju-osnovu/ . **Tariff amounts: not found.** Pilot-order route: tick "Подключение в рамках пилотного приказа" and attach the order [A] — https://teletype.in/@smartbridge/1zCOuhC8-iN .
- **ИБ testing (ГТС, Synaq portal):** five test types (source-code analysis, ИБ-function tests, load, network survey, ИБ-process survey); protocol valid 3 years but ≤1 year for production entry; 60 working days to remediate, two free re-tests within 20 working days [A] — https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V1900018795 , https://synaq.sts.kz/ . **Price list (Order 86/қе, version 1 Jan 2026, excl. VAT):** source code 5,812 ₸/MB; load test 410,910 ₸/use-case; network survey 745,079 ₸/subnet; ИБ functions 765,224 ₸/system; ИБ processes 740,344 ₸/system — i.e. roughly 2.7–3 M ₸ for a small system doing all five [A] — https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V1800017630 .
- **Registry/architecture portal:** state IS are registered on govarch.kz (Rules V1600013320 / V1900019104) [A] — https://govarch.kz/ru/register , https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019104 . Whether a *non-state* pilot system must be registered there beyond the agreed ТЗ: **not found**.
- **Timelines others report:** integrators advertise 2–4 weeks for 1–2 services and 1–2 months for 3–5 (vendor claims) [C] — https://smartconnect.kz/integraciya-egov , osn.kz above. The Rules themselves allow up to 3 months of testing; add 2–4 months for ИБ tests.

## 3. Authentication options for a third-party pilot

- **eGov QR (login + signing).** Citizen-side auth on egov.kz/idp.egov.kz is eGov QR, ЭЦП+SMS or ИИН/password+ЭЦП [A] — https://tengrinews.kz/news/egov-izmenili-sposobyi-vhoda-nujno-znat-kazahstantsam-586438/ , https://idp.egov.kz/ . For a third-party site the usable primitive is QR *signing* (NITEC-S-5096 or SIGEX): the returned CMS signature carries the НУЦ certificate with ИИН, so signing a challenge yields a verified ИИН without touching biometrics [B, inference from SIGEX docs: "аутентификация по цифровым сертификатам"]. Realistic for a pilot: **yes**.
- **OpenID Connect at idp.egov.kz for external relying parties:** **not found** publicly [C].
- **Digital ID** (NIT video-identification) is the eGov Mobile onboarding step, not an API offered to startups [A] — https://egov.kz/cms/ru/news/egov_biometrics . **ЦОИД** biometrics (КЦМР/NPCK) is restricted to financial organisations [B] — https://kisc-site.crocos.kz/udos-center/pcid/ , https://forbes.kz/articles/natsbank_zapustil_servis_udalennoy_biometricheskoy_identifikatsii . Not realistic.
- **Is a verified ИИН on a scam report legal?** Lawful with explicit consent and purpose, but it turns the report into personal data: localisation (art. 12), the Oct-2026 Rules (store PD exclusively in RK; masking of non-public data; deletion/anonymisation on demand) [A/B] — https://profit.kz/news/74963/ ; pseudonymised data is still PD, while anonymised/aggregated data is exempt from localisation [B] — https://akashi.cloud/ru/resources/guide/data-protection-law-kazakhstan/ . HMAC-hashed phone numbers are therefore most likely pseudonymised PD, not anonymised data — **needs a one-page legal opinion** [C].

## 4. Hosting and compliance for a real deployment

- **Localisation:** art. 12 since 2013 requires the PD database on RK territory; cross-border processing is not banned, but an RK-resident database is mandatory [A/B] — https://prg.kz/Document/?doc_id=39801023 . Railway abroad is not compliant once reports contain PD.
- **AI Law № 230-VIII**, in force 17–18 Jan 2026: risk classes minimal/medium/high and autonomy levels; Qorğan is minimal-to-medium risk, low autonomy (human decides). Obligations: inform users AI is used, publish a user agreement, continuous risk management and documentation, keep human oversight; synthetic-content labelling applies only if you generate content; fines via КоАП in МРП (amounts not found) [A/B] — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2500000230 , https://pro1c.kz/news/zakonodatelstvo/ii-v-kazakhstane-novye-zakony-obyazannosti-i-shtrafy/ , https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/v-kazahstane-vstupil-v-silu-novyiy-zakon-590541/ . Registration of minimal-risk systems: **not found**.
- **Digital Code № 255-VIII** (9 Jan 2026, in force 12 July 2026) unifies **experimental legal regimes (ЭПР)** and renamed Law 418-V to "О кибербезопасности" [A/B] — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2600000255 , https://prg.kz/document/?doc_id=33651101 , https://www.kt.kz/rus/state/v_kazahstane_poyavitsya_samostoyatelnyy_zakon_o_1377983845.html . - **Where to host:** NIT's ЦОД is the "серверный центр ГО" for state IS [B] — https://www.inform.kz/ru/v-kazahstane-otkryty-7-centrov-obrabotki-dannyh-nit_a2680551 ; QazCloud (Kazakhtelecom, built for state bodies, Kosshy), Kazteleport, PS Cloud Services (ISO 27001), Transtelecom, VK Cloud KZ are the commercial options [B] — https://serverspace.kz/about/blog/top-oblachnyh-provajderov-kazahstana/ , https://www.ps.kz/en , https://kazteleport.kz/ . - **Can a pilot run as an "ИС негосударственного лица" integrated via ШЭП?** Yes in law (art. 38, ЕТ para 2, Order 93/НҚ, pilot-order route) — provided a state body owns the use-case and signs. - **Where Qorğan adds value without duplicating:** everything above is number-, transaction- or link-based. Nothing analyses *what the caller says* in Kazakh/Russian, during or right after the call, on-device. The consented report is valuable only if it becomes a **structured input to existing channels**: an operator complaint (triggering the 2-working-day check), a potential-victim flag (Antifraud Center, via a participant), or evidence attached to a 102/"Закон и порядок" appeal. A fourth, cheap win: Nomad Guard already plans a number-reputation check — Qorğan's L2 co-occurrence graph is a candidate data source, not a competitor.

## 6. Platform reality for call analysis in a native app

- **Android:** third-party apps have had no access to in-call audio since Android 10; Play policy (May 2022) killed accessibility-based recording [B] — https://www.techradar.com/news/google-to-remove-many-call-recording-from-the-play-store-starting-tomorrow ; CallScreeningService is number/metadata only and InCallService needs the dialer role or companion-device permission [A] — https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/telecom . Google's conversational **Scam Detection** is Pixel-only, opt-in, needs a SIM *and* presence in one of 13 countries (no KZ, no RU/KK) [A] — https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/15654065 , https://blog.google/security/staying-one-step-ahead-strengthening-androids-lead-in-scam-protection/ ; Samsung S26 gets it in the US only [B]; "Fake call detection" (June 2026, global) verifies caller identity via RCS, not content [B] — https://9to5google.com/2026/06/02/google-phone-fake-call-detection/ ; Play Protect "Live Threat Detection" is malware, not calls [B] — https://security.googleblog.com/2024/05/io-2024-whats-new-in-android-security.html .
- **iOS:** Apple DTS: "no supported API" for a third-party app to access live audio of a call it is not carrying [A] — https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/841340 ; Apple's own call recording lists Russian as a language but Kazakhstan is absent from Call Recording/Transcription/Call Screening availability; Live Voicemail has no ru/kk [A] — https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/ .
- **WebView:** WKWebView exposes getUserMedia since iOS 14.3 only if the host app allows it, and the mic is muted when the app backgrounds [A] — https://webkit.org/blog/11353/mediarecorder-api/ , https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/689182 ; Android WebView needs the host's onPermissionRequest. So a Qorğan WebView inside eGov Mobile has exactly the PWA's capability: speakerphone, foreground only.
- **Realistic in-app shape:** (1) post-call "проверьте, что вам сказали" — paste/dictate/replay, score, explain, advise; (2) speakerphone live mode as an explicit foreground tool; (3) report with eGov-verified identity; (4) number lookup only as a *consumer* of Nomad Guard/AFC data, never as a self-built blacklist.

## (b) Three integration options

**Option 1 — WebView mini-service inside eGov Mobile ("Закон и порядок" → "Проверить разговор").**
Prerequisites: a state sponsor that owns the use-case (MVD or Prosecutor General for the section; MAIDD/КИБ for Nomad Guard adjacency); a pilot order; NIT dev slot. Checklist: ТЗ agreed with MAIDD; backend in an RK data centre; ИБ tests (all five types; protocol ≤1 year old at go-live); ТД ИБ, SOC/ОЦИБ contract; tripartite agreement (93/НҚ pattern) via Smart Bridge; mTLS certs; PD consent flow; AI-law notice. Timeline: 6–12 months after a sponsor signs (sponsor hunting is the long pole). Risks: NIT controls the roadmap and UI; 278 MB model inside a WebView is a hard sell (you will be asked to score server-side, which breaks the "nothing persists" invariant); foreground-only mic; overlap with Nomad Guard's planned number check; paid Smart Bridge tariffs unknown.

**Option 2 — Standalone PWA/app with eGov QR for verified reports.**
Prerequisites: move L2 to an RK host; consume NIT's QR-sign service (via Smart Bridge contract) or SIGEX as interim. Checklist: PD policy + consent texts (KK/RU); masking/anonymisation and deletion endpoints per the Oct-2026 Rules; AI-law user notice and agreement; legal opinion on HMAC digests; ISO 27001-style ТД ИБ even if not tested. Timeline: 2–4 months. Risks: discoverability without eGov; QR-sign tariff; still PD once ИИН is attached; no formal standing with MVD/AFC.

**Option 3 — Backend-only: consented reports → L2 → structured hand-off.**
Prerequisites: one participant partner (an operator or a bank already inside the Antifraud Center, or MVD) willing to receive structured reports. Checklist: export schema (number digest → operator complaint with timestamp, tactic tags, spans; AFC incident format); agreement on which party files the complaint; audit chain exposed to the partner. Timeline: 1–3 months for the pipeline; indefinite for the partner. Risks: AFC has no third-party ingress, so Qorğan is a data vendor to a participant; without a sponsor the reports go nowhere.

Recommendation: run 2 now (it is mostly compliance work you need anyway), use it to court a sponsor for 1, and shape every report around 3.
