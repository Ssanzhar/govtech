Readings:
- **EmbeddingGemma is a poor Kazakh encoder** despite "100+ languages": Tatoeba kaz-eng 0.27, NTREX 0.64, Belebele 0.68 — far below e5-base on every Kazakh task, and the technical report (https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20354) does not name Kazakh **[V]**. Its q4 (197 MB) is attractive only on size. Reject.
- **Nothing ≤300 MB beats e5-base on Kazakh**: gte-multilingual-base (340 MB int8) is below e5-base on the one Kazakh task it has (NTREX 0.904 vs 0.924) and has no classification/retrieval Kazakh scores; arctic-m-v2 is weaker on NTREX/Tatoeba. e5-small is **equal** to e5-base on Belebele (0.756 vs 0.757) and better on bitext, but you measured it worse on your own task — that stands.
- **The size budget is being spent on vocabulary, not on the transformer.** XLM-R-vocab models carry a 250,002×768 embedding matrix ≈ 192M of e5-base's 278M params; the encoder body is ≈86M **[E]**. Vocabulary trimming (Ushio et al., EMNLP 2023 Findings, https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15020; tool https://github.com/asahi417/lm-vocab-trimmer) retains performance at ~50% of the vocabulary **[V]**; a community kk-only trim of e5-small went 250,037→32,768 tokens, 117.6M→34.2M params, "70.91% smaller" (https://huggingface.co/alphaedge-ai/multilingual-e5-small-kaz-32768) **[V]**. For kk+ru+en keep ~80–100k pieces → e5-base ≈ 147–163M params ≈ **150–165 MB int8** **[E]**. This is the cheapest win: same encoder weights, same embeddings for all kept pieces (parity holds except where SentencePiece re-segments a rare word — measurable with the existing golden fixtures), half the download/RAM, and headroom. e5-large trimmed is still ≈400 MB **[E]** — out.
- Kazakh-native models: `issai/*` publishes no text-embedding model (LLMs, TTS, ASR only, https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=issai) **[V]**; `kz-transformers/kaz-roberta-conversational` is an 83.5M fill-mask MLM (no sentence objective) **[V]**; `Eraly-ml/KazBERT` variants exist but have no published benchmark **[U]**. Community evidence that e5-base adapts well to Kazakh: `shyngys879/kazakh-e5-rag-embedding` fine-tunes e5-base on KazQAD and lifts Hits@1 25.87→35.98 (https://huggingface.co/shyngys879/kazakh-e5-rag-embedding) **[V]**.

**Recommendation A1:** keep multilingual-e5-base; trim its vocabulary to kk/ru/en (new ADR; re-run parity fixtures and the browser gate); use the freed ~120 MB for a second small model only if A3 needs one. Do not move to e5-large-instruct (the only clear Kazakh upgrade, SIB-cls 0.824) — it does not fit WASM budgets even trimmed.

### A2. Fine-tuning recipes feasible on CPU / Apple Silicon

1. **SetFit (contrastive pair fine-tuning + LR head)** — Tunstall et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11055 **[V]**: with 8 labelled examples/class, SetFit-MPNet (110M) beats standard fine-tuning by 19.3 accuracy points on average and matches RoBERTa-large fine-tuned on the full 3k CR set; SetFit distillation into a 15M student gains +24.8/+25.1/+8.9 points (AGNews/Emotion/SST5) with 8 unlabelled samples (https://huggingface.co/blog/setfit) **[V]**. For Qorğan: pairs = (window_i, window_j) same/different class, ~1,400 dialogues → tens of thousands of pairs; a few epochs of e5-base at seq 512 on an M-series GPU is hours, not days **[E]**. Keep the `query:` prefix; the mE5 report trains with InfoNCE and a low temperature (https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05672) **[V]** — mirror that so the embedding geometry does not drift far from the pretrained one.
2. **Full / last-N-layer fine-tuning on 2–5k dialogues** — the literature says this is exactly what breaks under generator shift: a fine-tuned BERT on temporally split Chinese call data falls to **precision 0.128 / 0.041** (F1 0.223 / 0.077) (Wu et al., Findings of ACL 2026, https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1025.pdf, Table 2) **[V]**; KoBERT drops 98.5%→56.3% on an adversarial vishing set (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06180) **[V]**; GPT-4o paraphrases cut KorCCViD classifiers from 97.66% to 81.35% (https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16291) **[V]**. If you fine-tune the encoder at all, freeze ≥8 of 12 layers and gate on `shift`.
3. **LoRA → merge → ONNX int8** — PEFT `merge_and_unload` (https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/main/en/developer_guides/lora) then Optimum export and ORT `quantize_dynamic`. Dynamic quantisation quantises weights ahead of time from fp32 and activations at runtime (https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/performance/model-optimizations/quantization.html) **[V]**, so the int8 artefact is a deterministic function of the fp32 checkpoint **[E]**; the non-determinism lives in training (MPS vs CPU kernels). Export once, commit the hash, never re-export from a re-run.
4. **Distillation from LLM labels** — "Distilling step-by-step" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02301) **[V]** uses LLM rationales as a second training target; the Korean work used GPT-4o labels to LoRA-tune an 8B model that holds 94.6% on adversarial data where KoBERT holds 56.3% (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06180) **[V]**. For Qorğan the "teacher" is Claude writing per-utterance tactic spans and rationales in-session; the student is the LR/MLP heads. Scam Shield shows the cheap half of this works: 4 classic classifiers voting + an 8B model raises 0.87→0.90 and transfers 0.88 / 0.82 to two unseen SMS datasets (https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01746) **[V]**.

**Hard warning on parity:** every option 1–3 replaces the embedder. Heads are trained on the *browser's* embeddings (ADR D32/D33), so a new encoder invalidates every captured embedding, every `site/core` golden fixture and the 0/200 browser gate; budget a full Playwright re-capture (`npm run device:serve`) and a new ADR before training a single head.

### A3. Architectures that keep explainability

| Option | Evidence | Verdict for Qorğan |
|---|---|---|
| **Primitives + logic layer** (detect stable "semantic primitives" spans, combine with hand-written logic) | Wu et al. ACL-2026 Findings: F1 **0.718 / 0.578** vs BERT 0.223/0.077, vs attention-MIL 0.224/0.021 on SynthScamCall/RealCall (strict temporal split); within 0.03–0.13 F1 of a rule-guided LLM CoT at a fraction of the cost (Table 3); explicit span extraction is what makes it work (Table 5: 0.45→0.63) **[V]** | This is your hard-signal design, generalised: more cues, each with span extraction, verdict by logical combination. Strongest evidence found. |
| **Attention-pooling MIL over utterances** | Same paper: MIL stays at F1 0.09–0.26 across window sizes 10–100 (Table 9) because "the final verdict depends on their logic combination", not presence of a suspicious segment **[V]** | Confirms your measured utterance-head no-go. Skip. |
| **Hierarchical: utterance tactic posteriors → sequence model** | Vishing-Tactics-Bench (https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07151): utterance-level 6-TTP labels on 5,645 TeleAntiFraud calls; cascaded RoBERTa macro-F1 0.75; tactic *trajectory* is non-redundant for forecasting Financial Exploitation (AP@1 0.67) **[V]**. StreamFraudNet (https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20223): frozen encoder + windows + recurrent aggregation, ROC-AUC 0.9953 on a controlled English set, first prediction at 10 s, updates every 2 s **[V]** | Worth a small experiment: tiny GRU over your 15 per-utterance tactic posteriors + cue flags, call-level label. Explanation stays the tactic tags. |
| **Nearest-prototype over a scheme bank** | ProtoTEx (ACL 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05426): prototype-distance classifier matches BART-large on propaganda detection and yields faithful, user-helpful explanations **[V]** | Adds a scheme label "for free" and lets you add a new scheme by adding prototypes — but prototypes are generator-written; use only as the scheme layer, not the risk gate. |
| **Rule + embedding hybrid** | Your current model; the cue-anchoring paper is the published validation. TeleAntiFraud: fine-tuned audio-LLM detection F1 84.78 vs GPT-4o 50.00 (https://arxiv.org/html/2503.24115v4) **[V]** shows generic LLMs are not a free lunch either | Keep; grow the cue inventory per Part B. |

Cross-language/generator transfer reported: the Turkish S&P'26 poster (100 YouTube calls, 7 LLMs) finds transcripts beat raw audio for every model (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24523) **[V]**; the ScamBenchmark study finds LLMs generalise to unseen scams better than a fine-tuned BERT (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17353) **[V]**; ScamFerret is websites, not calls (0.972 accuracy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10110) **[V]**; ScamAgents is an *attack* paper (multi-turn agent scam scripts, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06457) **[V]** — useful as a second adversarial generator design, not as a detector. No paper reports Kazakh or Russian scam-call results **[V — none found]**.

### A4. Data strategy when the generator is Claude

The core problem: `shift` is already Claude-written, so once Claude writes training data `shift` is no longer independent (ADRs D35/D43 already forbid tuning on it; now even *reading* it as "honest" would be wrong).

**Concrete role swap (proposed):**
1. Freeze today's `shift` as `shift_v1` in the ledger; after the swap it becomes a *validation* split, never again the headline number.
2. Generator swap: the former training generator (Gemini) becomes the held-out generator — but its key sits in `.env` and you have no API budget, so cap it at a few hundred calls for a `shift_v2`.
3. **Third, local, free source**: run an independent model family offline on the Mac for held-out generation — `issai/LLama-3.1-KazLLM-1.0-8B-GGUF4` or `issai/Qwen3.5-4B-Kazakh` (https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=issai) **[V]** via llama.cpp. Different pretraining, different "things it likes to say", native Kazakh. Mark as `shift_local`.
4. **Real-world held-out**: public recordings of KZ/RU scam calls (journalist and victim uploads on YouTube/TikTok; the Turkish and "It Warned Me" papers both built sets this way **[V]**) transcribed with your own Vosk pipeline → `real_asr`, 50–100 calls, inspection-ledgered. This is the only split that also tests ASR noise honestly.
5. Human-authored negatives (team, volunteers) remain `authored_heldout`.

**Forcing diversity in Claude-written data (all with literature):**
- Grid, not prompts: scenario (Part B, ~20) × claimed role (~18) × language register (kk / ru / kk-ru switch / en) × victim persona (age, region, occupation; Persona Hub shows persona conditioning scales diversity, https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.20094 **[V]**) × outcome (complies / refuses / hangs up / calls back) × channel (cold call, call-back after SMS, messenger-then-call) × stage (hook / escalation / hand-off). AttrPrompt reports attributed prompts match class-conditional quality at 5% of the queries and that attribute diversity is what drives downstream accuracy (https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15895) **[V]**. Cosmopedia's topic × audience × style grid with dedup is the template (https://huggingface.co/blog/cosmopedia) **[V]**.
- Register the known limits: synthetic data helps least on subjective labels and its marginal value falls below 5% once ~1,000 real examples exist (https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07849) **[V]**; fine-grained persona detail adds little beyond coarse persona + an explicit length cutoff (EMNLP 2025 Findings, https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1146/) **[V]** — so vary length deliberately.
- Anti-mode-collapse prompting: Verbalized Sampling — ask for k candidates with probabilities, then sample (https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01171) **[V]**; the ACL-2026 paper finds small amounts of synthetic data help and the curve flattens fast (Fig. 3) **[V]** — do not generate 20k calls.
- Dedup/leak checks: Self-Instruct's ROUGE-L < 0.7 novelty filter against the pool (https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10560) **[V]**; e5 cosine near-duplicate removal within class (calibrate the threshold on your corpus — start where current train has no pairs > 0.92 **[E]**); a *generator-fingerprint probe* (train an LR to tell Claude from Gemini on scrubbed text — if it beats chance by a wide margin the style leak is real); a *lexicon-leak check* (require ≥30% of positives per scenario to contain **no** hard-signal cue, otherwise the embedding head never has to learn **[E]**); never extend the lexicon from any held-out split (D35/D43). Model-collapse background: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y **[V]**. TeleAntiFraud's three-source pipeline (ASR truth → TTS; self-instruct expansion; caller/callee/manager adversarial agents over a scenario × fraud-type grid) is the closest published blueprint (https://arxiv.org/html/2503.24115v4) **[V]**.

### B2. Roles the caller claims (observed)

Three current `impersonation_*` ids cannot carry 19 roles.

### B3. Proposed v2: three layers

**Layer 1 — tactics (span-level, multi-label; what the LR heads learn).** Keep urgency, fear_threat, secrecy, otp_request, credentials_request, safe_account, payment_redirect, remote_access (lexicon += screen-share/video-call), prize_lottery, investment_scam, mule_recruitment, verification_ploy. Replace the three `impersonation_*` with six: `imp_bank`, `imp_law_enforcement` (police/prosecutor/KNB), `imp_gov_service` (eGov/ЦОН/КГД/pension/clinic), `imp_regulator`, `imp_telecom_delivery_utility`, `imp_personal` (relative/boss/friend). Add five: `loan_inducement`, `cash_courier_handoff`, `refund_request`, `task_advance_fee`, `callback_instruction` ("press 1", "call this number back" — appears in #2, #21). Fold "complicity accusation" into fear_threat as a sub-signal. Negative classes += `legit_delivery_code`, `legit_refund_request`, `legit_employer_request`, `legit_clinic_appointment`, `legit_telecom_offer`, `legit_utility_notice` — these are the FPR guardrails for the new positives.

**Layer 2 — role slot (deterministic, lexicon-extracted, verbatim).** One of the 19 roles above, surfaced in the explanation as «Звонящий представился {role}» / «Қоңырау шалушы өзін {role} деп таныстырды». No learning; no FPR exposure.

**Layer 3 — scheme (call-level, single label, rule-derived).** The ~20 scenarios in B1, each a hand-written logical expression over Layer-1 tactics + role + stage (hook / escalation / extraction / hand-off, plus a multi-call flag) — exactly the primitives-then-logic design the ACL-2026 paper validates **[V]**. Adding a scheme = adding a rule and advice, not retraining heads, unless it needs a new tactic.

**Templates / advice changes.** Key advice by *scheme*, not tactic (today's advice_{ru,kk,en}.yaml is per tactic): add loans (never on instruction; eGov Стоп-кредит), cash hand-off (no official collects cash), refunds (only via the bank's in-app return), personal impersonation (hang up, call the known number, code-word), EDS/KGD (only egov.kz / pki.gov.kz / e-Salyq), delivery codes (only inside the app, never dictated). Reason templates gain the role slot and a stage line («Бұл сол легенда бойынша екінші қоңырау» / «Это уже второй звонок по той же легенде»). Reminder from CLAUDE.md: every lexicon addition above forces a retrain and must ship with training data; the generator grid in A4 should be built *from* this taxonomy so the two land in one ADR.
