How it works

Words in, verdict out.

01

Read

Paste a transcript or replay a call turn by turn. Everything stays in your browser — no upload, no cloud round-trip. On-device speech recognition is next.

02

Measure

A multilingual sentence embedding (e5, int8, on device) plus six interpretable signals: one-time-code request, "safe account", remote access, secrecy, card credentials — and the reassurance real banks give ("we never ask for your code").

03

Decide

A calibrated probability against an explicit threshold (0.59), a 0–100 meter with hysteresis during a live call, and the verbatim phrases that produced it. A human always decides.

Evidence

A verdict you can argue with.

Scores that can’t explain themselves get ignored — or worse, trusted blindly. Qorğan returns the evidence with every call: which tactics were detected and the exact phrases behind them, so a reviewing human can agree — or push back.

Every highlighted phrase is a verbatim span of the transcript, tied to a named tactic. Nothing is paraphrased by a language model.

Evaluation

False positives, reported first.

A detector that cries wolf gets switched off. Qorğan is tuned FPR-first, and every number carries its 95 % interval — a 0.000 on a small set is not a zero. The held-out set below is hand-written by the team, not real calls; a locked real-call set is the next step.
